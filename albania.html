Albania

A Country of Contrasts

Coast, culture and mountain air.

Begin by the bright waters of Ksamil, then follow the streets of Berat and Gjirokastër, where historic houses tell the story of everyday life across generations. Near the southern coast, Butrint adds a remarkable journey through the ancient past.

Travel north for the dramatic landscapes around Theth, or spend time discovering Tirana's cafés, landmarks and creative energy. With Liberty Incentive, you can bring together the places and experiences that speak to you.
