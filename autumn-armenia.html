Autumn in Armenia: Colors, Weather & Travel Tips

← Back to Travel Inspiration

Explore Armenia during its most colorful season, from lively Yerevan to the golden forests and monasteries of Dilijan.

Autumn is one of the most atmospheric seasons for discovering Armenia. Yerevan remains lively with open-air cafés, cultural events and relaxed city walks, while forests around Dilijan turn vivid shades of gold, orange and red.

Weather and what to pack

Temperatures vary by year and elevation, but these approximate Yerevan ranges are useful for planning:

September

Days 20–28°C · Nights 10–16°C

Warm, sunny and ideal for city walks.

October

Days 13–21°C · Nights 5–11°C

Cooler and richly colored.

November

Days 6–13°C · Nights 0–5°C

Crisp and quieter.

Seasonal highlights

Autumn brings grape harvesting, winery visits and seasonal food experiences.

Best places to visit in autumn

  • Yerevan
  • Dilijan and Haghartsin
  • Areni and Vayots Dzor
  • Lake Sevan
  • Lori, Haghpat and Sanahin
  • Garni and Geghard

What to pack

Pack layers, a light waterproof jacket, sun protection and comfortable walking shoes.

Temperatures are typical planning ranges, not a weather forecast.
