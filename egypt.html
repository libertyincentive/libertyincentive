A World of Contrasts

History and discovery at every turn.

Egypt brings together places unlike anywhere else: the monumental pyramids of Giza, Cairo’s lively streets and markets, and the temples of Luxor. Each destination reveals another chapter of the country’s rich heritage.

Along the Red Sea, Hurghada and Sharm El Sheikh offer warm sunshine, clear water and a different pace. Combine culture and coast in a journey designed around the experiences you enjoy most.

01

Ancient Wonders

Discover the pyramids, monumental temples and stories that have shaped Egypt for thousands of years.

Explore Cairo’s colourful markets, local flavours and the energy of everyday Egyptian life.

Red Sea Escapes

Slow down by the sea in Hurghada and Sharm El Sheikh, with beaches and water activities to enjoy.
