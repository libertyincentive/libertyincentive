Beyond the Skyline

Many sides of one remarkable destination.

Begin with Dubai’s dramatic skyline and its lively waterfront, then slow down among the wind towers and narrow lanes of Al Fahidi Historical Neighbourhood. Beyond the city, open dunes invite you to experience the desert at a different pace.

In Abu Dhabi, the Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque and Louvre Abu Dhabi offer memorable encounters with architecture, art and culture. Whether you prefer vibrant streets, thoughtful discoveries or time beside the sea, we can shape the journey around you.
