Winter in Armenia: Snow, Mountains & Adventure

← Back to Travel Inspiration

Discover skiing, ropeway rides, snowy landscapes, festive Yerevan and memorable mountain escapes across Armenia.

Winter reveals a different side of Armenia, combining snowy mountain scenery with festive city life. Yerevan glows with seasonal decorations, welcoming restaurants and cultural attractions throughout the colder months.

Typical winter temperatures

These approximate Yerevan ranges are a guide:

December

Days 0–8°C · Nights -5–0°C

Festive lights and early snow.

January

Days -2–4°C · Nights -8–-3°C

Usually the coldest month.

February

Days 0–7°C · Nights -6–-1°C

Winter sports continue.

What to do in winter

  • Ski or snowboard in Tsaghkadzor.
  • Try guided snow activities.
  • Explore festive Yerevan.
  • Visit Gyumri.
  • See Lake Sevan or relax in Jermuk.
  • Combine cultural sites with indoor experiences.

Planning a comfortable winter journey

Snow quality and resort operations vary through the season, so check conditions before committing to ski days.

What to wear and how to prepare

Bring thermal layers, an insulated waterproof jacket, gloves, a hat and waterproof shoes.

Temperatures are typical planning ranges, not a weather forecast.
