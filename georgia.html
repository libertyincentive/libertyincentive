Georgia

Explore Georgia ↓

Where Europe Meets Asia

One journey, countless contrasts.

Georgia brings remarkable variety into a compact country. Medieval churches rise above mountain valleys, lively neighbourhoods surround Tbilisi’s historic centre, and vineyards stretch across the sunlit landscapes of Kakheti.

Its greatest impression often comes from the people: warm welcomes, shared meals, expressive music and a strong sense of tradition turn every visit into a personal experience.

01

Ancient Heritage

Sacred landmarks, historic capitals and mountain churches tell stories stretching back through centuries.

02

Living Traditions

Wine, cuisine, polyphonic music and generous hospitality remain part of everyday Georgian life.

03

Changing Landscapes

Snow-covered Caucasus peaks, green valleys, vineyards and the Black Sea create extraordinary variety.

Places to Experience

Discover Georgia’s many stories.

01

Tbilisi

Explore colourful balconies, historic sulphur baths, hilltop fortresses and an energetic creative scene in Georgia’s capital.

02

Mtskheta

Discover Georgia’s ancient spiritual capital, where treasured churches stand at the meeting point of two rivers.

03

Kazbegi

Follow the Georgian Military Highway to spectacular alpine scenery and the iconic Gergeti Trinity Church.

04

Gudauri

Enjoy sweeping Caucasus panoramas and mountain adventures in Georgia’s best-known high-altitude resort.

05

Kakheti

Experience Georgia’s celebrated wine region through vineyard landscapes, traditional cellars and welcoming local tables.

06

Batumi

Meet the Black Sea in a lively coastal city known for its long boulevard, subtropical gardens and modern skyline.
