Forests & Monasteries

Dilijan

A green mountain retreat shaped by forests, craftsmanship and quiet monasteries.

← Back to Discover Armenia

Often called Armenia’s little Switzerland, Dilijan offers fresh mountain air, wooded trails, artistic traditions and some of the country’s most atmospheric medieval monasteries.

Forests & Monasteries

A town embraced by forest

Dilijan lies within Dilijan National Park, where dense woodland, mineral springs and mountain valleys create a softer, greener landscape than much of Armenia. The town developed as a health retreat and creative center.

Its restored old quarter recalls nineteenth-century architecture and artisan life, while nearby Haghartsin and Goshavank monasteries connect the region’s natural beauty with Armenia’s spiritual and educational heritage.

Slow down in Dilijan

Spend the morning walking through forest, stop for tea or coffee in the old quarter, meet a local craftsperson and continue to a monastery hidden among the trees. Dilijan rewards travelers who leave room for silence and unhurried discovery.

Mountain hospitality

Enjoy homemade soups, herb-filled dishes, local cheeses, mushrooms, preserves and seasonal produce. Family-run guesthouses and restaurants give the region a warm, personal character.

When to visit

May to October is ideal for walking and nature. Autumn is especially beautiful as the forests change color. Winter is quiet and atmospheric, though hiking conditions and road access should be checked.

A local travel tip

Do not limit the visit to a quick monastery stop. An overnight stay allows time for a forest walk, the old quarter and Dilijan’s relaxed evening atmosphere.
